1. Recording rule

The B1, B2, B3… series identifies experimental versions and does not replace normative versioning of the Language. A Beta may be public and functional before becoming part of the stable realization.

Stable incorporation of a Beta is recorded separately from its experimental existence and does not remove proposals or debts that remain open.

Incorporated capabilities

  • Explicit SVP-ES/SVP-EN selection, without autodetection.
  • One internal computational identity shared by both source profiles.
  • Assembly of independent-profile units without concatenating source text or lexical sequences.
  • Editor, example library, diagnostics, security, features and metrics in the public environment.
  • WebAssembly identity verified by byte length and hash.

Inherited debt not attributable to B2

B2 incorporation does not by itself resolve the inherited grammatical debt recorded as DFL-007. That debt belongs to the sovereign Language front and keeps R2 continuity suspended until its correction and corresponding recertification.

5. Continuity status

Beta B2 remains the experimental antecedent of the stable realization incorporated on 29 August 2026. Later Betas must retain their own identity, reproducible evidence, explicit limits and a traceable relation with any future stable incorporation.
