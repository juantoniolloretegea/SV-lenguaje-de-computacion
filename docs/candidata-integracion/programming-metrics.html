1. B2 relative performance

Against 2A, B2 showed no attributable dynamic-memory regression and no Spanish-source-profile-specific penalty. Under the tested full load, the measured compilation-time variation was approximately +0.85% for EN and +0.65% for ES. These figures do not represent CPU utilization percentages.
