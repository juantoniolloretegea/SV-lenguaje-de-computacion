Experimental state. Beta B2 presents diagnostics emitted by the compiler; it does not redefine their identifiers or normative meaning.

1. Applicable catalogue

The B2 reference version is f6b704e819e7d08589f00e9e3f9550deab21d0b1. For Grammar 0.2 and IR 0.3, the public reference document is docs/referencia/ERRORES_CANONICOS_SV_v0_3.md.

That effective catalogue contains 50 codes. Version 0.3 preserves the 0.2 catalogue and adds E110, E305 and E308.

3. Compiler/interface separation

E... codes belong to the compiler catalogue. Browser messages concerning WebAssembly loading, source-size limits, UTF-8, hashes or transport are Beta-interface conditions and must not be confused with semantic diagnostics of the Language.

B2 preserves the structured output returned by WebAssembly and does not assign an alternative meaning to catalogue codes.

4. Known divergence

The public catalogue explicitly preserves a historical divergence for E204: the historical IR identifies it as MissingConflictOperator, while the effective initial-compilation catalogue uses it as QueryMissingContext. B2 neither hides nor locally rewrites that divergence.

5. Scope of this page

This page provides access from the Beta. The exhaustive definition of each code, its emission points and maintenance obligations remain in the reference document of the main Language repository.
